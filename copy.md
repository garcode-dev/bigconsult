                          FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT IS CAC REGISTRATION
CAC registration is simply the process of getting your business, company or
organization registered with CAC (the body responsible for regulating and
incorporating businesses in Nigeria)

WHY DO I NEED TO DO CAC?
Basically, registering your business with CAC means your business becomes a legal
entity that will be recognized by the nigerian government, and this would give your
customers more reasons to trust that you are not a scam.

WHAT DOCUMENTS DO I NEED TO REGISTER MY BUSINESS?
Generally, you will need government-issued IDs for all directors/proprietors, proof of
address, signature samples, and your proposed business names.

HOW LONG DOES THE INCORPORATION PROCESS TAKE?
Name availability checks usually take 24–48 hours, while full business name or
limited liability company registration is typically completed within 7 to 14 business
days.

AS A FOREIGNER, CAN I FULLY OWN A BUSINESS IN NIGERIA?
Yes, foreigners can fully incorporate and own a limited liability company, though
certain sectors require specific regulatory permits or minimum share capital
thresholds.

HOW DO I KNOW IF MY BUSINESS REQUIRES SCUML?
SCUML stands for Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering. It is legally
required for Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs) such as
real estate, hotels, law firms, jewellers, and consultants to monitor and prevent
money laundering.

So if your business falls under any of these, you’ll need a SCUML certificate

You will need your incorporation documents (CAC status report/documents), Tax

Commission, but it must be activated with the tax authorities before you can file
